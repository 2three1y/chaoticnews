About Chaotic News

Unbiased. Unverified. Unhinged. Serving readers since last Thursday.

Our Mission

Chaotic News Network was founded last Thursday with one goal: to report the news before it happens, and, where possible, instead of it happening. We are a satire site. Every story, quote, expert, statistic and correction on this website is made up.

Our newsroom runs on three principles: be first, be loud, and if anyone asks, it was Steve.

Our Standards

  1. Every story is checked by at least zero fact-checkers.
  2. All anonymous sources are named Steve, for consistency.
  3. We never accept money from advertisers, mostly because none have asked.
  4. If a headline sounds true, we apologize. That was an accident.
