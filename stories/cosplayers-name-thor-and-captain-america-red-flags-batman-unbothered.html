LONDON COMIC CON — Dexerto posted a video on X tonight, and superheroes everywhere are having a rough evening.

What Actually Happened

Dexerto went to London Comic Con and asked cosplayers to judge each other’s favorite superheroes. The verdict, in Dexerto’s words: “Homelander, Thor and Captain America all got red flags.”

That’s the news. It’s a short video, not a long article, so the source for this one is Dexerto’s post itself, linked at the bottom. We are not going to make up which red flags the cosplayers named. You can watch the video and hear it from them.

What we can say: Homelander getting a red flag is the least shocking result in comic con history. He is basically a walking red flag in a cape. Thor and Captain America, though? Two of the most famous good guys around, flagged by the people who know them best: the fans who dress up as them.

Our Take (This Part Is Made Up)

Our dating desk tried to reach Batman for comment. He did not answer. Sources close to the cave say he has not left it to find out what anyone thinks, and does not plan to. He is reportedly “unbothered,” “brooding on schedule” and “busy.”

A local cosplayer dressed as a sandwich told us nobody has ever red-flagged a sandwich. “I’m loyal, I’m reliable, and I come with chips,” he said. Sadly, he was not in the video.

Experts remind everyone that red flags are a matter of opinion, and that every hero in this story is fictional, except the cosplayers, who were great.
