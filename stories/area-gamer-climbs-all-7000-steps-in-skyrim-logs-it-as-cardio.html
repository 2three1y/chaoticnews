Area Gamer Climbs All 7,000 Steps in Skyrim, Logs It as His Cardio for the Year

There aren’t really 7,000 steps up Skyrim’s Throat of the World. A Bethesda art director says they tried to fit them all, and the path ended up near Whiterun.

HIGH HROTHGAR — PC Gamer posted on X that a Bethesda art director really did try to fit 7,000 actual steps into Skyrim’s famous 7,000 Steps. Fitness apps around the world braced for impact.

What Actually Happened

In Skyrim, when the Greybeards call you to High Hrothgar, a messenger named Klimmek in the village of Ivarstead warns you that the climb up the Throat of the World takes the 7,000 Steps.

There are not 7,000 steps. Some are buried under snow, but the real count is closer to 700. Fans at the UESP, a big Elder Scrolls fan wiki, counted about 2,400 footsteps to walk the whole thing.

But the team did try. As part of Bethesda’s 40th anniversary, the studio’s associate art director, who refined the mountain and dressed it with plants, effects and the steps, explained that they did their “darndest” to fit all 7,000 in. Their best attempt went way past Ivarstead, wrapped around the mountain and ended near Whiterun in the frozen tundra. “It turns out, 7,000 is quite a lot!” they wrote.

They also added that they did not place the enemies, so they take “no responsibility for the Frost Troll.” Every Skyrim player who got flattened on that path just felt a little closure.

PC Gamer’s point: open-world games shrink distances on purpose, so the trip doesn’t take a real week.

Our Take (This Part Is Made Up)

A local gamer climbed the whole path today, controller in hand, and logged it in his fitness app as his cardio for the year. His watch disagrees. It recorded 41 steps, all of them to the fridge. He says the Frost Troll counts as interval training.
