Man Offering to Buy Audiobook Rights to Your Fanfic Admits He Never Had a Studio, He Just Really Needed to Know How Chapter 47 Ends

The so-called producer had no studio, no microphone and no money. He did have a pillow taped to a closet wall and very strong opinions about the cliffhanger.

By Penny Popup, Consumer Affairs Correspondent

SOMEWHERE ONLINE (CN) — A man who spent eight months messaging fanfiction writers with offers to buy the audiobook rights to their stories admitted Monday that he never owned a recording studio, never had any money, and was “only ever trying to find out how chapter 47 ends.”

The man, who signed his messages “Rex Goldvoice, CEO, Goldvoice Audio Universe Studios Global,” contacted an estimated 312 writers, all of whom received the same offer: “big deal, big money, sign here.” Investigators say 311 of those messages were a distraction. Only one writer mattered.

“I said ‘big deal, big money,’” Goldvoice told Chaotic News Network from a beanbag chair. “But all I wanted was chapter 48. It has been eight months. Do you know what that does to a man? I check for updates during dinner. I check during the dentist. I checked during my cousin’s wedding vows.”

Inside the “Studio”

Investigators who visited Goldvoice Audio Universe Studios Global found a hallway closet containing one pillow taped to the wall, a microphone shaped like a karaoke microphone because it was a karaoke microphone, and a sign reading “QUIET: RECORDING” that had been written on the back of a pizza box.

The studio’s sound-proofing consisted of the pillow and “my mom knowing not to open the door,” Goldvoice said. His mom opened the door during our interview to ask if we wanted grilled cheese.

His contract, which several writers received, was a screenshot of chapter 47 with the word “PLEASE” written across it in red, and a signature line labeled “sign here and also post ch 48.”

“We knew something was off,” said Detective Lydia Plothole of the Online Fraud Unit. “Real audiobook producers don’t open with ‘so do they kiss or not.’ Real producers don’t ask for the next chapter as a ‘sample.’ And real producers don’t start crying in the voice memo.”

The Offer, Explained

Goldvoice’s pitch evolved over time. CN reviewed his messages and found a clear progression:

  1. Month one: “I will turn your story into an audiobook for $50,000.”
  2. Month three: “I will turn your story into an audiobook for $50.”
  3. Month five: “I will read chapter 48 out loud for free if you write it.”
  4. Month seven: “I will pay YOU to tell me if Kael survives.”
  5. Month eight: “Is Kael okay. Just yes or no.”

Like every audiobook offer in a stranger’s DMs, the deal also required writers to pay a “$99 processing fee” first. Goldvoice said this was “just to make it seem real” and that he had “no idea how to process anything.”

“I don’t want the rights. I want the chapter. I want closure. I want to know about Kael.”

The Writer Speaks

The author of the fanfic, who posts under the name moonlight_quill_77, says she is flattered by the attention and has no plans to change her schedule.

“I’m not posting chapter 48 until I feel it,” she said. “Could be next week. Could be 2029. The chapter knows when it’s ready. Also I have a biology test.”

She added that she had received 14 messages from Goldvoice under different names, including “Audiobook Larry,” “Big Book Money LLC,” and “Definitely Not Rex.”

What Experts Say

Experts are reminding writers that real publishers do not slide into your messages asking you to pay them first.

“If someone offers you big money for your fanfic, then asks you to send them money, that’s a scam,” said Professor Harold Cliffhanger of the University of Please Update. “Block and report. And if they ask whether two characters kiss, that’s not a scam. That’s just a fan. Still block them, though.”

Goldvoice says he has learned his lesson and will now “wait patiently like a normal reader.”

At press time, he had started writing chapter 48 himself. Early reviews describe it as “not good” and note that Kael survives by becoming a helicopter.
