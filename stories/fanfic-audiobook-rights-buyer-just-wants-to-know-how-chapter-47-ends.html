Man Offering to Buy Audiobook Rights to Your Fanfic Admits He Never Had a Studio, He Just Really Needed to Know How Chapter 47 Ends

The “producer” had no studio, no microphone and no money. He did have strong opinions about the cliffhanger.

By CN Staff, Scams Desk

Updated 7:18 PM EST, Mon November 2, 2026, give or take · 1 min read (blink and you’re done)

Satire, Obviously. Chaotic News is satire. All stories are made up. Do not forward this to your aunt as fact.

SOMEWHERE ONLINE (CN) — A man who messaged hundreds of fanfiction writers offering to buy the audiobook rights to their stories has admitted he never owned a studio. He says he just really, really needed to know how chapter 47 ends.

“I said ‘big deal, big money, sign here,’” he told Chaotic News. “But all I wanted was chapter 48. It has been eight months. Do you know what that does to a man?”

Investigators say his “studio” was a closet with a pillow taped to the wall. His “contract” was a screenshot of the story with “PLEASE” written on it in red.

The writer of the fanfic says she is flattered, but still not posting chapter 48 until she “feels it.”

Experts remind writers: real publishers don’t slide into your messages asking for money, and they definitely don’t ask “so do they kiss or not.”

Chaotic News stands with fanfic writers everywhere. Never pay anyone for a deal. Block and report.

At press time, the man had started writing chapter 48 himself. It is reportedly “not good.”
