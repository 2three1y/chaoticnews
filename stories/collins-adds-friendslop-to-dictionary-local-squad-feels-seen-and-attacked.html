Collins Adds “Friendslop” to the Dictionary. Local Squad With 600 Hours in the Same Co-op Game Says It Feels Seen and Also Attacked

The Collins dictionary added “friendslop,” the word for cheap, chaotic co-op games you play with friends. “Mogging” beat it for Word of the Year.

THE GROUP CHAT — IGN posted on X that “friendslop” has been added to the Collins dictionary. Every Discord server with a channel called #game-night felt a sudden chill.

What Actually Happened

The Collins dictionary added a batch of new words, a lot of them from Gen Z and Gen Alpha slang. Its official definition:

“friendslop: a type of low-budget, independently produced video game, designed to be played casually with a group of friends.”

In other words: the cheap, messy co-op games you play with your squad until 3am, yelling the whole time. IGN lists Peak, Gamble With Your Friends, Lethal Company and R.E.P.O. as big ones, with millions of sales on Steam (and a lot of copycats on Roblox). IGN also says Big Walk, which it gave a 9 out of 10, is arguably the best friendslop game of the year.

Friendslop did not win, though. Collins picked ”mogging” as its Word of the Year. Mogging means outshining someone at something: height mogging, wealth mogging, looks mogging, you name it. So friendslop got mogged by mogging, which is a sentence we can now legally print.

Another new word on the list: “fricy,” which apparently means food that is both fruity and spicy.

Our Take (This Part Is Made Up)

We visited a local squad with 600 hours in the same co-op game. They say the definition “feels seen and also attacked,” especially the “low-budget” part, since they each paid seven dollars and consider it the best money they have ever spent.

One member has asked Collins to add “one more round” as a unit of time. Its official length: about four hours.
