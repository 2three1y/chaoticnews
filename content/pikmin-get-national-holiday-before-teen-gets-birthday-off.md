Tiny plant creatures known as Pikmin officially have their own holiday, while an area teen whose birthday is next week has been told he still has a math test that day.

“They’re like an inch tall,” the teen said. “They don’t even go to school. What did they do to earn a holiday?”

Records show the Pikmin carried roughly 40,000 pieces of fruit across a cartoon garden this year, mostly while being thrown by a tiny astronaut. The teen carried one backpack to school and complained about it.

“Honestly, the little guys are cooking,” admitted a vice principal who asked not to be named. “Have you seen them work? We should all be more like the Pikmin.”

The teen has filed a formal request for “Area Teen Day,” to be celebrated with no homework and a large pizza. The school board has agreed to “look into it,” which experts say means no.

At press time, the teen was reportedly painting himself red and lining up behind a tiny astronaut, hoping it would count.
