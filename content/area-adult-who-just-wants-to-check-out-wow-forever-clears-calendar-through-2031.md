[IGN](https://x.com/IGN) posted the launch times for World of Warcraft Forever on X this morning, and an entire generation of adults quietly opened their calendars and started deleting things.

## What Actually Happened

Blizzard confirmed the global release time for World of Warcraft Forever: **November 4 at 3pm Pacific**, which is 6pm Eastern. It is a global launch, so everybody on Earth starts at the same moment. Depending on where you live, that moment lands on November 4 or November 5. No region gets a head start, which is great news for fairness and terrible news for anyone who planned to "just peek at it first."

You can preload it right now. Open the Battle.net launcher, and WoW Forever should be sitting in your Favorites bar. If it isn't, go to Games, search for World of Warcraft, pick WoW Forever under "game version," and hit the big blue Install button.

Now the scary part. Blizzard's requirements say you need at least **128GB of free space**, and they specifically call for an SSD, not an old hard drive. IGN suggests saving around 150GB, because WoW players install add-ons the way other people collect fridge magnets.

## Our Take (This Part Is Made Up)

Our reporters reached a local adult who said he only wants to "check it out." He has since cleared his schedule through 2031, renamed the family dog "Healer," and told his kids he will be back right after one more dungeon. That was in 2019, in a different game. The pattern is strong.
