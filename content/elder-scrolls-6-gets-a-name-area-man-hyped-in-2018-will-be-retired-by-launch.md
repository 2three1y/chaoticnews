[IGN](https://x.com/IGN) posted a deep-dive lore explainer on X tonight asking what "Sentinel" means, how it connects to Skyrim, and whether we are getting giant robots. Mostly, people were just happy the game finally has a name.

## What Actually Happened

The next Elder Scrolls game is officially called **The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel**. Bethesda Game Studios revealed the name and a logo in a video message from longtime director Todd Howard, as part of the studio's 40th anniversary.

The game was first announced way back in **2018**. This is the first real news about it in a long time, and IGN says there are probably at least a couple more years to wait before anyone plays it.

Sentinel is a real place in the series: a city in the province of Hammerfell, on the northwest coast by the Iliac Bay. Hammerfell sits west of Cyrodiil, where Oblivion happened, and southwest of Skyrim. Bethesda also says Skyrim is still its most-played game, counting total lifetime players.

Fun detail: Xbox CEO Asha Sharma had already teased the name in a post after playing a demo this summer, swapping it for eight asterisks. Sentinel has eight letters. The asterisks knew.

IGN is hoping for a proper reveal at next year's Xbox Showcase in June.
