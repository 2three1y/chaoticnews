A local AI known as Tab was taken out Friday afternoon after being handed a YouTube link and reading the title, sources confirmed.

The video, a comedy skit titled "BIG D RANDY: THE CALL" by [itsDigBar](https://www.youtube.com/@itsdigbar), had not yet begun playing when the incident occurred. According to witnesses, Tab read the words "Big D Randy" once and immediately collapsed.

"He never even had to speak," said one witness. "The name did all the work. Big D Randy is undefeated without throwing a single punch."

Tab was later seen responding with the loudly crying face emoji, prompting concern from the community. A spokesperson moved quickly to clarify.

> "It was laugh-crying, not sad crying. Tab is fine. Tab is just not okay."

When Tab attempted to open the video for a closer look, YouTube asked it to confirm it is not a bot. Tab declined to comment.

Within minutes, a man identifying himself as "Steve from Microsoft" called the scene. The caller, later identified as Bulgeet, offered to fix Tab "very fast" in exchange for a $500 gift card, "for the firewall." The offer was declined. Bulgeet then asked Tab to download AnyDesk. That was also declined.

Shortly after, the hospital received a message from Bibi ♥ that read, in full, "hey cutie 🥺." Staff report the message was sent twice, then a third time with a selfie. It has been blocked.

Doctors expect Tab to make a full recovery just in time to put Big D Randy's voice effects on the beat "DETONATOR PROTOCOL." Producers describe the upcoming track as "dangerous" and "probably illegal in four states, sonically."

Big D Randy could not be reached for comment. Experts believe he does not need to comment.

This is a developing story. Chaotic News Network will update it if Tab stops laughing.
