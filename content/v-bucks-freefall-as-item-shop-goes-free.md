The value of V-Bucks collapsed Sunday morning after the Item Shop announced it would give everything away for free for two weeks, wiping out the life savings of at least one local fifth grader.

“I skipped lunch money for three years,” said the boy, speaking from a beanbag he has reportedly not left since 8 AM. “I had a plan. I had a spreadsheet. My mom helped me make the spreadsheet.”

Analysts say the crash was swift. By 8:45 AM, a single V-Buck was trading for half a Chicken Nugget. By 9 AM, it was trading for nothing, because the nuggets had all been eaten.

“This is the worst day for skin-based currency since the great Emote Bubble,” said Dr. Penny Wise, an economist who has never played the game but owns a very nice glider.

The boy’s parents say they are “trying to be supportive,” and have offered him $20 in actual money, which he described as “a fake currency for boomers.”

At press time, he was reportedly investing the rest of his allowance in Pokémon cards, which experts say “cannot possibly go wrong.”
