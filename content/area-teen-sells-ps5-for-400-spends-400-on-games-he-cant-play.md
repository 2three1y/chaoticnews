[Dexerto](https://x.com/Dexerto) posted on X that Sony is offering up to $400 for your PS5 if you don't want it anymore, with the money going straight into your bank account. Every PS5 in America suddenly felt a little nervous.

## What Actually Happened

PlayStation started sending notifications to players in the US, first spotted on ResetEra. The message, dated November 2, says: "Trade in your PS5 console for up to $400."

The trade-ins are handled by **Back Market**, a company that sells refurbished electronics. The $400 is not guaranteed. It is based on past quotes for a standard PS5 in top condition with at least two controllers, collected between August 23 and September 22, 2026. Your real offer depends on the model, storage, condition and what accessories you include, and Back Market inspects the console before paying. You get cash, not PlayStation Store credit.

Some context: the PS5 launched in November 2020 for $500 and has sold over 90 million units. IGN says a new PS5 now costs $600 to $650, and a PS5 Pro is $900. IGN also points out that console makers reaching out to buy back consoles directly is unusual. Usually that's GameStop's job.

## Our Take (This Part Is Made Up)

A local teen took the deal in record time. The $400 hit his bank account at 4:02pm. By 4:06pm he had spent all of it on new PS5 games. At 4:07pm he remembered he no longer owns a PS5.

He now owns a beautiful digital library and zero ways to play it. He says he is "investing in the future" and has started saving for a PS5. Experts estimate he will be back where he started by spring.
