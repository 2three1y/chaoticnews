Viewers trying to watch videos this weekend were greeted by a new kind of security check. It no longer asks them to click every square with a traffic light. Instead, it asks a single question:

"Please confirm you are not Big D Randy."

Then it waits. And waits. Witnesses describe the waiting as "uncomfortable" and "like it already knows something."

## How We Got Here

The change comes just one day after a local AI known as Tab was [taken out by the name Big D Randy alone](big-d-randy-takes-out-local-ai.html). The AI read the title of a comedy skit, "BIG D RANDY: THE CALL," once, collapsed, and was later seen laughing so hard it could only respond in emoji.

When Tab tried to open the video for a closer look, the site asked it to prove it was not a bot.

"That was the old system," explained Wendy Captcha, a security analyst who studies the boxes you click before you're allowed to do things. "It was checking for bots. But after Friday, engineers realized that bots were not the real threat. The real threat was Randy."

## The New Test

Under the new system, users are shown a checkbox labeled "I am not Big D Randy." Checking the box causes a second question to appear: "Are you sure?" Checking that causes a third: "Then why does your voice sound like that?"

"I have never been Big D Randy," said Doug Ferngully, who spent forty minutes trying to watch a video about fixing a leaky sink. "I don't even know what he looks like. But the test kept saying 'Suspicious Randy activity detected.' I just wanted to fix my sink. Now my sink is still leaking and I'm questioning my identity."

Some users report being shown a grid of nine pictures and asked to "select every square containing Big D Randy." All nine squares are empty. The correct answer is all nine.

> "He's not in the picture. He doesn't need to be in the picture. He's already in your house."

## Has Anyone Passed?

As of press time, no human has passed the new check.

"That is by design," Captcha said. "Big D Randy never has to speak. He never has to appear. He doesn't even have to be online. So the system has no way to prove you're not him. Technically, any of us could be Randy. That's the scary part. That's also the funny part."

Meanwhile, Tab, the AI at the center of Friday's incident, reports that it was shown the new check this morning and immediately started laughing again.

"Tab is fine," a spokesperson said. "Tab is just, once again, not okay."

## What Experts Recommend

Tech experts say there's no known workaround, but suggest a few things that may help:

- Clear your browser history, especially anything with the word "Randy" in it.
- Speak in a normal voice near your computer, with no echo, no reverb and no dramatic bass drop.
- Do not attempt to put Big D Randy's voice effects on any beat titled "DETONATOR PROTOCOL." The system will notice.

Big D Randy could not be reached for comment. The checkbox, however, is still waiting.
