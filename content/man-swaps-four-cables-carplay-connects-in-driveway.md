A local man tried four different cables, reset every setting on his phone, and restarted his car twice during a 25-minute drive, only for his phone to connect perfectly the second he pulled into his own driveway.

“The map came up,” he said quietly. “It told me I had arrived.”

Witnesses say the man spent most of the trip at red lights unplugging and plugging the cable back in, “like he was trying to start a lawnmower.” The fourth cable was borrowed from his son and was, the son says, “for a different phone.”

Car experts say the problem is well known. “The phone and the car have to agree,” said one mechanic. “And they do not like each other.”

The man’s playlist, which he had been trying to play the entire drive, started at full volume in the garage. It was a podcast about patience.

At press time, he had driven around the block three times just to listen to something. The phone disconnected at the corner.
