An area teen discovered Monday that pressing his phone’s side button twice sends a call straight to voicemail, and used the trick on his own mother within four minutes.

“I read it in a tech article,” the teen said. “I thought, wow, I will use this wisely.” He did not.

His mother was calling to ask if he wanted pizza. She has since left a 3-minute voicemail, which the teen also sent to voicemail by accident while trying to listen to it.

“I could hear the button,” his mother told Chaotic News. “Click click. I know that click. I invented that click. I gave birth to that click.”

Phone experts warn that the double press is a powerful tool and should be used only on robocalls, car warranty calls, and anyone named Steve from Microsoft.

The pizza, which had pepperoni, has been eaten by the teen’s younger sister, who answered on the first ring.

At press time, the teen had learned a second trick: what happens when your mom changes the Wi-Fi password.
