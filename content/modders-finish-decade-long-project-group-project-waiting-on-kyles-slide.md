A team of volunteer modders finished a project ten years in the making this week, releasing complete tools for a classic space horror game. Meanwhile, an area teen’s group project, assigned the very same year, is still waiting on one slide from Kyle.

“We all did our parts in like a day,” the teen said. “Slide 4 just says ‘KYLE PUT YOUR STUFF HERE.’ It has said that for a decade.”

The modders say they worked nights and weekends, kept careful notes, and answered every message. Kyle has read every message in the group chat. He has replied to none of them.

“He did react with a thumbs up once,” the teen said. “In 2019.”

The teacher who assigned the project has since retired, moved to Florida and taken up pottery. The due date has been extended 46 times.

Reached for comment, Kyle said, “Oh, that’s due?”

Productivity experts say the lesson is clear: with enough time and enough passion, people can build anything, as long as none of them is Kyle.
