An area teen spent six straight hours Monday carefully power washing every inch of a virtual pink dream house, while his actual bedroom floor remains buried under a layer of hoodies first reported in August.

“The DreamHouse had grime on the hot tub,” the teen explained. “I couldn’t just leave it.”

His mother says the bedroom floor has not been seen in months. “I think it’s carpet,” she said. “It was carpet. I believe it is still carpet.”

The teen insists the two are not the same. “In the game there’s a percentage bar,” he said. “When I clean my room, nothing tells me I’m at 87%. Where’s the satisfying sound? Where’s the little chime?”

Researchers confirm the room does not have a chime.

At press time, his mother had placed a power washer sticker on the vacuum cleaner. The teen reportedly approached it, stared at it for several minutes, then went back to the hot tub because he had missed a spot.
