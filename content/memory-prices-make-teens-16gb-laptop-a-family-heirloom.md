With computer memory prices reaching what experts call “unprecedented” and “honestly kind of rude” levels, an area teen’s 16GB laptop has been officially appraised as a family heirloom.

“It’s got a crack in the corner and a sticker of a frog on it,” the teen said. “Apparently that makes it vintage.”

The appraiser, who arrived wearing white gloves, said the laptop was worth “more than the car, less than the house, and about the same as the dog.”

The family has moved it to a glass case in the living room. The teen is now allowed to use it for 30 minutes a day, supervised, with no snacks within six feet.

His grandmother has already asked who gets it in the will. “I want the RAM,” she said. “The rest you can split.”

Tech analysts say prices could keep rising. Families are advised to check their attics for old computers, because Grandpa’s 2009 desktop might be worth a boat now.

At press time, the teen had opened 40 browser tabs, and the whole family held their breath.
