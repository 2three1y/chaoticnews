[Xbox Wire](https://x.com/XBOXWire) posted on X that The Silver Seas, the latest expansion for Age of Wonders 4, is out now, "loaded with pirates, sharks, and more things players have been asking to see." The sharks would like it on the record that they asked first.

## What Actually Happened

Age of Wonders 4 from Triumph Studios is entering its fourth year and its fourth set of expansions, which the studio says makes it the longest-supported game in the series. The Silver Seas came straight from player requests.

The big addition is the new **Mercantile Culture**, which lets you build cities at sea and turn them into floating fortresses. There are two flavors of pirate: the **Cartel**, who grow through smuggling and secrecy, and the **Barony**, who throw money around, put bounties on enemies and import whatever they need.

There are also two new seafaring peoples. The **Aqualon** started as a piece of concept art the studio once shared, and fans liked it so much the team brought it back. The **Sharkkin** exist because, the studio says, every time it asked players which new people to add, a healthy portion of the answers was just "Sharks!"

On top of that: a Magic Tome built on water magic, plus ice magic, new pirate dwellings, pirate stories and terrifying sea creatures. No vapor magic this time. The studio also says the expansion finally gave it an excuse to write a sea shanty at work.
